# Hairven — Booking Platform

Customer booking platform for Hairven, built around the entire hair experience — not just appointments.

Brand: warm, welcoming, feminine, modern, premium-but-affordable, fun and youthful. Classy • Cute • Unique.

## What it does

* **Explore Hairstyles:** browse by category (e.g. Braids, Wig & Frontal, Natural Hair, Locs, Custom), search by name, filter by price, length, colour, style type. Each style has photos, starting price, description, add-ons, and Request This Style.
* **Tell Ese What I Want:** custom requests with name, inspiration photos, description, hair details, special requests, preferred date/time. Supports combos (e.g. colour from one photo, length from another).
* **Consultation (free):** online via WhatsApp or physical. Integrated into the request flow.
* **Pricing:** starting prices shown; final price agreed in consultation based on length, size, colour, fullness, add-ons, extensions/materials. Slightly negotiable.
* **Appointment scheduling:** customers see availability but can also suggest a preferred time. Ese confirms or suggests an alternative. Finalised only on mutual agreement.
* **Deposits & payments:** 50% deposit only after style, customisation, extensions, final price, and date/time are agreed. Bank transfer, debit/credit card, and other Nigerian options. Balance paid in-studio.
* **Summary & receipts:** pre-payment summary (style, add-ons, extension choice, total, deposit, balance, date/time, location, policies, status). Email confirmation + receipt after payment.
* **Cancellation / rescheduling:** shown before payment. Customer cancel: 50% of deposit refunded. Customer reschedule/no-show: fee applies, 48h notice. Ese cancels: 100% refund. Ese reschedules: no penalty.
* **Accounts (optional):** guest browsing/booking supported. Accounts add loyalty points, faster booking, member promos, upcoming/past appointments, favourites, inspiration, consultation/payment history.
* **Save / share / recently viewed:** save to phone, save to My Favourites (account), share via WhatsApp, Recently Viewed section.
* **Gallery vs Customer Looks:** professional Gallery (work, before/after, studio) separate from Customer Looks (real customers, uploads, ratings/reviews).
* **Reviews:** 1–5 stars after completed service, text + photo optional. Selected reviews featured on homepage with permission.
* **Support:** branded support bot for booking, pricing, deposits, policies, prep, etc., with clear Talk to a Person escalation.
* **Notifications:** WhatsApp (quick/consultation), Email (confirmations/receipts), SMS (reminders/urgent). Reminders days-before, day-before, day-of with prep instructions.
* **Visit Us:** address, hours, contact, map/directions (details TBD).
* **Ese's management side:** single-owner management of requests, consultations, availability, pricing, payments, catalogue, promos, gallery, reviews, support. Role-based access planned for future team.

## Customer flow

Discover → explore/search/filter → choose style or custom request → add photos/details → consultation → agree customisation/extensions → agree final price → agree date/time → finalised → pay 50% deposit → secured → reminders + prep → studio visit → pay balance → review → loyalty/return.

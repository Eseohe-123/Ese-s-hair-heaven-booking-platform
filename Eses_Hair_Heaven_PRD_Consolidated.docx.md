**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)**

**Ese’s Hair Heaven**

Product: Ese’s Hair Heaven Website / Customer Booking Platform

Status: Initial Product Definition

Primary Owner: Ese

Primary Users: Customers of Ese’s Hair Heaven

Brand Personality: Warm, Welcoming, Girly/Feminine, Modern, Premium but Affordable, Fun and Youthful

Brand Words: Classy • Cute • Unique

**1\. Product Vision**

Ese’s Hair Heaven is being created around the customer’s entire hair experience, not simply as a “pick a hairstyle, book an appointment and come to the salon” platform.

The experience should take a customer from discovering inspiration, choosing or describing what they want, getting personalised guidance, agreeing on the hairstyle and price, finalising an appointment, paying the required deposit, receiving reminders, getting their hair done, leaving feedback, and returning for future services.

The long-term vision is for Ese’s Hair Heaven to grow from a hair-styling business into a broader hair brand, with future product sales and potentially multiple stylists.

**2\. Brand Direction**

The brand should feel warm and welcoming, girly/feminine, modern, premium but affordable, fun and youthful.

Core brand words: Classy • Cute • Unique.

The visual identity should use a warm, cute and distinctive colour direction. The exact colour palette is still to be developed.

A logo has not yet been created. An existing flyer designed for Ese’s Hair Heaven can be used as inspiration for the future logo and visual identity.

The previous phrase “Everything Hair” may be refined or replaced because it led people to assume the business mainly sells hair. The final tagline/message should communicate the broader hair experience.

**3\. Homepage**

The homepage should immediately communicate what Ese’s Hair Heaven offers and create a warm, classy, cute, unique and modern impression.

Primary action: Explore Hairstyles.  
Secondary action: Tell Ese What I Want.

The homepage can also highlight featured hairstyles, occasional promotions, selected customer reviews/photos and other important parts of the customer experience.

**4\. Hairstyle Catalogue**

Customers can browse hairstyles organised into categories.

Working examples include Braids, Wig & Frontal, Natural Hair, Locs and Custom Hairstyle. These will be replaced or updated with Ese’s actual services.

Customers can search by hairstyle name and filter by useful options such as price, category, length, colour and style type.

Each hairstyle has a detailed page with photos, name, starting price, description, customisation/add-ons and a Request This Style option.

**5\. Hairstyle Requests & Inspiration**

Customers can either choose an existing hairstyle or tell Ese what they want.

A custom request can include a hairstyle name, one or more inspiration photos, a description in the customer’s own words, hair details, special requests and preferred appointment date/time.

Customers can explain combinations across multiple inspiration photos, such as using the colour from one photo, length from another and curls from another.

**6\. Consultation**

Consultations are free.

The customer clicks the Consultation button on the website and is taken to the Ese’s Hair Heaven WhatsApp page to begin the consultation.

Consultation options are:  
• Online consultation through WhatsApp  
• Physical consultation

The consultation should be integrated into the hairstyle-request experience and should not create unnecessary waiting or extra steps.

**7\. Pricing**

Starting prices are displayed for hairstyles rather than treating every displayed price as fixed.

Final price can vary based on:  
• Length  
• Size  
• Colour  
• Fullness  
• Add-ons  
• Extensions/materials supplied by Ese’s Hair Heaven  
• Other special requests

Prices are slightly negotiable and can be discussed during consultation. Final pricing must be agreed before the appointment is finalised and before the deposit is paid.

**8\. Add-ons & Customisation**

Customisation is an important part of the service.

Possible options include extra length, extra fullness, different colour, curls, hair accessories, wash and treatment, and other available add-ons.

The final cost of selected options is confirmed during consultation.

**9\. Hair Extensions**

Customers can either bring their own extension or get extension/materials from Ese’s Hair Heaven.

If Ese provides the extension, its cost is added to the final bill.

During consultation, details such as colour, size, amount, type/quality and price are discussed and agreed before the appointment is finalised.

**10\. Appointment Scheduling**

Customers can view available time slots and unavailable dates/times, but they can also submit their own preferred date and time.

Ese remains in control of availability. If a requested time works, Ese confirms it; if not, Ese suggests an alternative.

The appointment is finalised only after both the customer and Ese agree on the date and time.

**11\. Deposit & Payments**

The customer pays a 50% deposit only after the hairstyle, customisation, extensions/materials where applicable, final price, appointment date and appointment time have been agreed.

The deposit secures the appointment.

Payment options will include bank transfer, debit/credit card and other convenient Nigerian payment options.

The remaining 50% is paid at the physical studio.

**12\. Appointment Summary & Receipts**

Before deposit payment, the customer sees:  
• Hairstyle  
• Add-ons  
• Extension choice  
• Total price  
• 50% deposit  
• Remaining balance  
• Appointment date/time  
• Studio location  
• Consultation details  
• Cancellation/rescheduling policy  
• Appointment status

After payment, the customer receives confirmation and a payment receipt by email.

**13\. Cancellation & Rescheduling**

Customer cancellation: only 50% of the deposit is refunded; the other 50% is retained.

Example: ₦50,000 service → ₦25,000 deposit → ₦12,500 refund → ₦12,500 retained.

Customer rescheduling: a rescheduling fee applies. At least 48 hours’ notice should be provided.

No-show: customer may reschedule, but a rescheduling fee applies.

Ese’s Hair Heaven cancellation: customer receives a 100% refund of the deposit.

Ese’s Hair Heaven rescheduling: customer pays no additional penalty or fee.

The policy must be clearly shown before deposit payment.

**14\. Customer Accounts**

Account creation is optional. Customers can still browse, request hairstyles and book as guests.

Account benefits include:  
• Loyalty points  
• Faster booking  
• Special member-only promotions  
• Upcoming and previous appointments  
• Saved favourite hairstyles  
• Saved inspiration  
• Consultation history  
• Appointment management  
• Payment history  
• Customer history

The account should feel like a benefit, not a requirement.

**15\. Saved Styles, Phone Saving & Sharing**

Anyone can save a hairstyle to their mobile phone.

Customers with accounts can save hairstyles to My Favourites and revisit them for future inspiration.

Customers can also share hairstyles directly from the platform, especially through WhatsApp and other available sharing options.

**16\. Recently Viewed**

Customers should have a Recently Viewed section so they can quickly return to hairstyles they recently viewed.

**17\. Loyalty & Promotions**

Ese’s Hair Heaven will have a loyalty programme. Agreed account benefits include loyalty points, faster booking and special member-only promotions.

Promotions will run occasionally rather than continuously.

Customers can be notified through email, SMS and WhatsApp Status.

Possible promotions include new-customer offers, member promotions, birthday offers, loyalty offers, seasonal promotions, special hairstyle promotions and referral offers.

**18\. Gallery**

The professional Gallery showcases Ese’s Hair Heaven’s work, including different hairstyles, lengths, sizes, colours, before-and-after looks, finished hairstyles, creative/custom styles and studio photos.

**19\. Customer Looks & Reviews**

Customer Looks is separate from the professional Gallery and can showcase real customers, finished hairstyles, customer-uploaded photos, reviews and customer experiences.

Customers can leave a 1–5 star rating after a completed service. Written feedback and finished-hairstyle photos are optional.

Selected customer reviews/photos can be featured on the homepage with the customer’s permission.

Ese’s Hair Heaven can also create and use its own customer hairstyle photos with appropriate permission.

**20\. Customer Support Bot**

The platform should have a friendly, personalised customer-support bot that provides instant replies.

It can help with booking, hairstyles, starting prices, consultation, deposits, payments, extensions, cancellation, rescheduling, studio information, appointment preparation and other common questions.

The bot should feel like part of the Ese’s Hair Heaven brand, not a generic robot.

**21\. Human Support Escalation**

Customers should always have a clear “Talk to a Person” option.

The bot should direct customers to Ese or customer-care personnel when the issue is urgent, the bot cannot resolve it, the customer asks for a person, or personalised assistance is required.

**22\. Notifications & Reminders**

Ese’s Hair Heaven will use WhatsApp, email and SMS.

WhatsApp is for quick communication, consultation and important updates. Email is for confirmations, receipts, appointment information and records. SMS is for short important reminders and urgent updates.

Appointment reminders should be sent a few days before, the day before and on the appointment day.

Reminders can include hairstyle, date, time, studio location, outstanding balance and preparation instructions.

**23\. Appointment Preparation & Contact**

Customers should receive preparation instructions before appointments, including whether hair should be washed, whether to bring extensions, what to bring, arrival information and other relevant instructions.

Customers should also have a simple “Contact Ese” option from their appointment information.

**24\. Customer History**

Relevant customer history should be available, including previous hairstyle requests, consultation discussions, previous appointments, saved styles, payments, reviews, preferences and booking history.

This helps returning customers receive a more personalised experience and makes repeat bookings easier.

**25\. Visit Us**

A dedicated Visit Us section should include the studio address, opening days, opening hours, contact information, map/directions and important visitor information.

Exact studio details will be added later.

**26\. Ese’s Management Side**

For now, Ese manages the entire platform.

Ese should manage customer requests and history, consultations, availability, appointments, pricing, deposits/payments, hairstyles, categories, add-ons, extensions/materials, promotions, reviews, Gallery, Customer Looks and support conversations.

Ese can confirm appointments, suggest alternatives, reschedule and cancel appointments.

**27\. Future Team Access**

When a social media/customer-care manager is hired, they can be given access to the areas they are responsible for. Ese remains in control of important business decisions.

**28\. Future Growth**

Future phases can include selling hair, hair extensions, hair-care products, accessories and other hair-related products.

The platform should also leave room for multiple stylists if the business grows beyond Ese alone.

Product sales are a future phase and are not required for the initial launch.

# **33\. Corporate Finance, Governance & Company Law**

The platform and business operating model should support appropriate Nigerian corporate, financial and governance controls as Ese’s Hair Heaven grows. These requirements should remain largely behind the scenes so the customer experience stays simple.

## **33.1 Legal Structure & Ownership**

* Define and document the legal structure of Ese’s Hair Heaven before launch or any incorporation changes.  
* Maintain clear records of CAC details, ownership, directors and applicable beneficial-ownership information.  
* The platform should distinguish the owner from the business as separate legal/financial identities where applicable.  
* Future shareholder, investor or ownership changes should be supported by proper corporate documentation.

## **33.2 Business and Personal Money**

* Separate business funds from personal funds where the business structure requires or supports a separate legal identity.  
* Distinguish customer receipts, owner capital, owner withdrawals, reimbursements, business expenses, taxes, supplier payments and staff payments.  
* Owner withdrawals should not be recorded as ordinary business expenses.

## **33.3 Owner/Director Payments**

* Distinguish salary/remuneration, expense reimbursement, director/shareholder loans, capital introduced and dividends.  
* Director/shareholder loans and related-party transactions should be properly documented, authorised and recorded.  
* Profit distributions should follow applicable corporate approval and accounting requirements.

## **33.4 Bank Accounts & Payment Reconciliation**

* Customer payments should settle into the appropriate business payment/bank account.  
* Reconcile booking records with payment-provider records, bank settlements and accounting records.  
* Record payment-provider charges separately from gross customer payments.  
* Management reporting should distinguish gross receipts, fees, refunds, net settlements and outstanding balances.

## **33.5 Customer Payment Classification**

* Link every customer payment to the relevant appointment.  
* Distinguish appointment advances/deposits, final service payments, extension/material payments, refunds, cancellation charges, discounts, credits and other adjustments.  
* Distinguish money received from revenue recognised where accounting or tax treatment requires different treatment.  
* Financial records must remain retrievable and must not be silently deleted or overwritten.

## **33.6 Price Agreement Before Payment**

* Before deposit collection, show the agreed hairstyle, specifications, add-ons, extensions/materials, total price, deposit, balance, appointment details and cancellation/rescheduling terms.  
* Once final price and deposit are agreed, Ese’s Hair Heaven should not unilaterally increase the agreed price.  
* Customer-requested additional work or materials should be separately priced and approved before charging.  
* Undisclosed mandatory extras should not be added at payment.

## **33.7 Appointment Deposit**

* The current commercial model is a 50% advance payment after hairstyle, specifications, final price and appointment date/time are agreed.  
* Provide a record of amount paid and outstanding balance.  
* Do not hard-code the accounting treatment of an advance payment as immediate revenue.

## **33.8 Cancellation by Customer**

* Display the cancellation policy before deposit payment.  
* Cancellation charges should be clearly disclosed, reasonable and consistently applied, subject to applicable Nigerian consumer-protection law.  
* Record amount paid, cancellation charge, amount retained and amount refundable.  
* Do not hard-code the current example of retaining exactly 50% of the deposit until the final legal/commercial policy is confirmed.

## **33.9 Cancellation by Ese’s Hair Heaven**

* If Ese’s Hair Heaven cancels before providing the service, refund amounts paid for the affected appointment unless the customer elects to reschedule.  
* No cancellation or rescheduling charge should apply where the change is initiated by Ese’s Hair Heaven.

## **33.10 Rescheduling**

* At least 48 hours’ notice is the current business requirement, subject to the final published policy.  
* Carry an existing deposit to the rescheduled appointment rather than requiring a duplicate deposit.  
* Display any rescheduling fee separately.  
* Link the original and rescheduled appointments in the records.  
* No additional penalty should apply when rescheduling is initiated by Ese’s Hair Heaven.

## **33.11 No-Show**

* Record no-show separately from cancellation.  
* Disclose the applicable no-show/rescheduling consequence before payment.  
* Show any applicable fee or outstanding amount before a new appointment is secured.

## **33.12 Refund Processing**

* Where practicable and appropriate, refund through the original payment method/account.  
* Refunds to another account require appropriate verification.  
* Record request date, reason, original payment, refundable amount, retained amount, approval, method, reference and status.  
* Use statuses such as Requested, Approved/Rejected, Processing and Completed.  
* Customer credit may be offered where appropriate but should not remove or restrict a refund right required by law.

## **33.13 Payment Errors & Failed Booking**

* Handle duplicate payments, overpayments, failed payments, payment-success-but-booking-failure and payment-provider reconciliation issues.  
* Do not require a customer to pay again while a successful payment is being verified.  
* Identify and resolve duplicate or excess payments separately.

## **33.14 Payment Disputes & Chargebacks**

* Retain records needed to investigate disputed or unauthorised payments.  
* Relevant records may include booking details, price agreement, terms acceptance, invoice/receipt, payment reference, communications, cancellation records and refund history.  
* Only authorised personnel should manage payment disputes and refunds.

## **33.15 Service Complaints & Remedies**

* Separate cancellation/refund rules from complaints about the quality or conformity of a completed service.  
* Maintain a documented complaint and remedy process for material differences from the agreed service.  
* Possible remedies may include correction/rework, partial refund, full refund where appropriate or another agreed remedy, subject to applicable law and circumstances.  
* Retain consultation notes, agreed specifications and relevant customer-approved inspiration.

## **33.16 Discounts, Promotions & Loyalty**

* Retain original price, discount/promotional amount and actual agreed selling price.  
* Calculate refunds using the actual transaction and applicable terms.  
* Reverse or adjust loyalty points when the underlying transaction is refunded or cancelled according to programme rules.  
* Keep promotional credits and purchased customer credits separately identifiable.

## **33.17 Financial Approval & Authority**

* Use role-based permissions for refunds, discounts, price changes, payment adjustments, expenses, financial reports and customer-data exports.  
* Customer-care personnel may submit refund requests without necessarily having approval authority.  
* Large or exceptional refunds and material adjustments should require higher-level approval.  
* Record who made/approved each financial adjustment, when, what changed and why.

## **33.18 Fraud Prevention & Audit Trail**

* Do not silently delete or overwrite financial records.  
* Changes to prices, discounts, refunds, cancellation charges, payment status or financial records should retain original value, revised value, date/time, user and reason.  
* Restrict financial information according to role.

## **33.19 Accounting & Financial Reporting**

* Maintain appropriate accounting records and a defined financial reporting process.  
* Support management reporting for profit and loss, financial position, cash flow, customer/supplier balances, refunds, deposits/advances, inventory, assets and liabilities as applicable.  
* Support periodic reconciliation and management reporting as the business grows.  
* Maintain corporate accounting records and statutory filings in accordance with applicable Nigerian company law.

## **33.20 Expenses, Vendors & Withholding**

* Record business expenses separately from customer transactions.  
* Retain vendor identity, invoice/receipt, Tax ID where applicable, amount, date, purpose and payment evidence.  
* Assess whether withholding-tax obligations apply to particular vendor/service-provider payments.  
* Document employee and independent-contractor relationships and treat them according to their actual legal relationship.

## **33.21 Staff, Stylists & Contractors**

* Document whether each future stylist is an employee, independent contractor, commission-based stylist or another arrangement.  
* Agreements should address compensation, working arrangements, customer relationships, confidentiality, intellectual property, equipment, content/photo rights, customer solicitation, termination and disputes.  
* Management access should be role-based; stylists should not automatically receive unrestricted financial or customer-data access.

## **33.22 Customer Ownership & Business Database**

* Treat customer records, booking history, consultation history and payment history as business records subject to applicable privacy/data-protection requirements.  
* Distinguish the customer’s relationship with Ese’s Hair Heaven from the stylist assigned to an appointment.  
* Future stylist contracts should address use of customer information and customer solicitation after departure.

## **33.23 Intellectual Property**

* Document ownership or rights in the brand name, logo, website, domain, social-media accounts, photographs, videos, written content, marketing materials and booking platform.  
* Contracts with developers, designers, photographers and marketers should clearly address intellectual-property ownership/licensing and permitted use.

## **33.24 Assets & Inventory**

* Record business assets with ownership, purchase cost and location where appropriate.  
* Future inventory should distinguish extensions/materials consumed in services from products sold directly to customers.  
* Future product sales should have separate inventory, pricing, revenue and applicable tax treatment.

## **33.25 Loans, Investment & Future Shareholders**

* Record loans, owner funding, investor funding and equity investment as distinct categories.  
* Document any future investor/shareholder arrangement before accepting funds in exchange for ownership.  
* Preserve financial history sufficient for future financing, investment, expansion or valuation.

## **33.26 Corporate Compliance Calendar**

* Maintain a calendar for applicable CAC filings, annual returns, corporate records, beneficial-ownership/PSC updates, tax filings, financial statements and other statutory obligations.  
* Update corporate information when directors, shareholders, registered address, ownership or control changes.  
* Confirm specific filing dates and obligations against current law and regulator guidance.

## **33.27 Business Continuity & Backups**

* Maintain fallback processes for bookings, payment verification, customer contact and appointment records if the website or payment system is unavailable.  
* Back up customer and financial records and ensure authorised recovery is possible.  
* Keep critical booking and payment information accessible during outages.

## **33.28 Succession & Exit Planning**

* As the business grows, operational continuity should not depend solely on one person’s informal access.  
* The company structure and agreements should provide a basis for future succession, sale, ownership transfer, investment or other exit arrangements.

# **34\. Tax, Regulatory & Consumer-Protection Compliance**

The business shall maintain compliance processes appropriate to its legal structure, activities, turnover, assets and applicable Nigerian law. The platform should remain flexible because tax and regulatory treatment may change as the business grows.

* Maintain applicable CAC registration/business information and Tax ID records.  
* Support applicable invoice/receipt requirements and sequential transaction numbering where required.  
* Support applicable VAT treatment rather than assuming all transactions are taxable or exempt.  
* Retain tax-relevant transaction and vendor records in retrievable form.  
* Support applicable electronic invoicing/fiscalisation requirements if introduced or required.  
* Review tax status periodically as turnover, assets, services, product sales, staffing and legal structure change.  
* Maintain clear customer-facing pricing, cancellation, refund and service-remedy terms consistent with applicable consumer-protection requirements.  
* Maintain privacy notices, marketing permissions and customer-photo/review permissions for personal information and content collected through the platform.

# **35\. Financial & Customer Payment Data Model — Minimum Records**

| Record | Minimum Information | Purpose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Appointment | Customer, hairstyle, specifications, date/time, status | Links service to financial activity |
| Price Agreement | Original price, customisations, extensions/materials, discount, final price, customer approval | Commercial record |
| Deposit | Amount, date, method, provider/reference, appointment | Tracks advance payment |
| Final Payment | Balance, date, method, provider/reference | Closes appointment balance |
| Refund | Reason, amount, retained amount, approval, method, reference, status | Refund control |
| Cancellation | Who cancelled, date/time, reason, applicable charge | Applies policy |
| Reschedule | Original appointment, new appointment, fee, deposit carried forward | Prevents duplicate charging |
| Discount | Original price, discount, final price, authorisation | Promotion control |
| Vendor Payment | Vendor, invoice, Tax ID where applicable, amount, date, purpose | Expense/tax control |
| Audit Log | Original value, revised value, user, date/time, reason | Fraud/dispute control |
